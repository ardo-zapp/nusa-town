# Nusa Town Asset Manifest & Ownership Log

This document serves as an official record of asset ownership, contributor attribution, and the history of asset removal for the Nusa Town project. Our goal is to ensure full transparency during the transition to a commercially viable and independent project.

## Current Project Status: TRANSITIONING
Nusa Town is currently in a "Cleansing Phase."

- **Code:** Public Domain (Independent).
- **Assets:** Mixed (Transitioning from CC BY-NC 4.0 to Proprietary).
- **Goal:** 100% original assets by LandBurner and team before official commercial release.

*Note: Any assets not yet listed in Section 1 or Section 2 are still considered legacy assets and are treated according to their original non-commercial licenses until replaced.*
